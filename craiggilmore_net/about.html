Biography

38 Years
in the Industry

Thank you for your interest in my work and welcome to my official website. I am a 38-year veteran comic book creator and storyboard artist actively producing work worldwide for the motion picture, television, and video game industries, as well as advertising agencies and major comic book publishers.

I am currently based in Bolivia, NC and normally prefer to work from my home studio. However, I have a studio kit that is completely mobile for extended on-location as well as remote television and motion picture productions worldwide.

With regards to the movie and television projects I take on, I am interested in conceptual illustration, poster illustration, and live action shooting boards — meaning quick and sketchy, with most of the work focusing on storytelling.

I am a proud I.A.T.S.E. union artist. I am represented for personal appearances and guest speaking engagements by Pearl Talent Management — please contact Brittany Byrum at brittanyb.ptmgmt@gmail.com.

You can also find my film and television credits on IMDb.

Work With Craig

Commission Info

Availability

Availability is based on Craig's current comics, film, or TV show workload. Turnaround time is generally 1–2 months, and you will be kept updated on the status of your piece throughout.

Rates

Craig uses a sliding scale that starts between $300 (black & white) and $1,000 (full color), based on your request factors. Commissions are normally completed on 11×14 bristol board unless a specific size is requested.

Payment

Payment is due when the piece is completed — PayPal or Square only. Pre-payment in person is accepted at shows. It's always okay to ask questions; Craig will do his best to work with you.

Convention Sketches

Craig sells pre-drawn original sketches at his table at conventions, and may maintain a post-show sketch list for pieces completed at home. Live sketching at shows is limited — check with him at the table during slower periods.
