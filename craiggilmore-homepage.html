About

38 Years
in the Industry

I am a 38-year veteran comic book creator and storyboard artist actively producing work for the motion picture, television, and video game industries, as well as advertising agencies and major comic book publishers.


Based in Bolivia, NC, I work from my home studio with a fully mobile kit for on-location and remote productions. I am a proud I.A.T.S.E. union artist, represented for personal appearances by Pearl Talent Management.
